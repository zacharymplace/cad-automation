# ZCAD Engineering Verification 001

## Result

VERIFIED

## Claim

ZCAD has sufficient bounded digital engineering evidence
to support an evidence-ready capability claim.

## Verified evidence

- Stable documented core/product architecture boundary.
- 471 zcad-core tests passed.
- 310 cad-automation tests passed.
- Package compatibility contract passed.
- 44 focused Pico 2 geometry tests passed.
- Pico 2 body and lid are valid modeled solids.
- STEP engineering exports exist.
- STL manufacturing exports exist.
- Manufacturing metadata records material, printer profile,
  tolerances, dimensions, volume, and estimated mass.
- Manufacturing artifacts are SHA-256 identified.

## Boundary

This verification covers digital CAD engineering evidence.

It does not prove:

- physical body-to-lid fit
- physical Pico 2 mounting alignment
- physical display or cable clearance
- slicer correctness
- successful physical printing
- production readiness

The existing decision remains:

**Ready for one bounded test print.**
