# Pico 2 Physical Fit Trial 001

## Purpose

Validate the existing Pico 2 enclosure engineering package
through one controlled physical print and fit review.

## Source evidence

- bounded-engineering-package-001
- engineering-verification-001

## Required observations

- slicer profile
- material
- layer height
- body print result
- lid print result
- body-to-lid fit
- Pico 2 mounting alignment
- display clearance
- cable clearance
- visible defects
- dimensional or fit deviations

## Decision

Pending physical test.

Allowed outcomes:

- accept
- revise
- reject

## Boundary

No production-readiness claim is permitted from this trial.

A successful result proves only the tested configuration,
printer, material, slicer settings, and physical sample.
